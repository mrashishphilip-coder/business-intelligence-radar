#1 · ENTERPRISE AI
Deep dive recommended

AI applications may win by owning the work between systems of record

The strongest enterprise-AI opportunity may not be adding intelligence inside Salesforce, SAP or Oracle. It may be taking responsibility for the messy cross-functional work that produces the final record.

Novelty 9.4Evidence 8.7Confidence 8.6Relevance 9.8
What changed: Recent operator conversations converge on the idea that AI-native products can capture negotiation, exception handling, document interpretation and policy judgment that previously lived in email, spreadsheets and meetings.

Disagreement: The open question is whether these agents become standalone systems or are absorbed by incumbent workflow vendors.
